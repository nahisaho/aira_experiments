# Search Strategy

## Objective
Identify recent peer-reviewed studies and reviews informing CFPS productivity optimization, energy regeneration, transcription-translation coupling, ion optimization, Bayesian optimization, and membrane protein nanodisc workflows.

## Databases and APIs
1. ToolUniverse MCP attempt using Semantic Scholar search tools (failed/partial due to 400/429 responses).
2. Semantic Scholar Graph API fallback via Python requests.
3. Crossref Works API for DOI completion and additional screening.

## Search Queries
- cell-free protein synthesis optimization ODE model
- CFPS energy regeneration system comparison
- cell-free transcription translation resource competition
- Bayesian optimization cell-free protein synthesis
- membrane protein cell-free expression nanodisc
- cell-free protein synthesis magnesium potassium optimization

## Eligibility Criteria
**Inclusion:** studies or reviews relevant to CFPS productivity, energy regeneration, biochemical resource coupling, ion composition, design/optimization, or membrane-protein/nanodisc implementation; publication year 2020 or later for the primary evidence set; articles with DOI when available.

**Exclusion:** off-topic cell-free metabolic engineering without protein synthesis focus, papers lacking sufficient metadata, duplicate records, and clearly pre-2020 records for the primary screened set.

## Screening Process
Single-reviewer title/abstract screening with documented limitation, followed by metadata extraction to a structured table.

A modular biodegradable-polymer design workflow was implemented to couple hydrolysis prediction, mechanical tradeoff optimization, enzymatic degradation, marine simulation, combinatorial copolymer search, and polymer-specific case studies. The hydrolysis benchmark achieved best cross-validated R² = 0.925 ± 0.037 (95% CI [0.878, 0.971]). The mechanical module identified 39 Pareto-optimal formulations, while the enzyme module showed that Cutinase gave the fastest PHA degradation and reached 56.58% degraded fraction by day 30. Ocean Monte Carlo simulation predicted surface-zone PHA mass loss of 99.9999996897641% at 365 days (95% CI [99.9999971352870, 99.9999999999966]). The highest desirability composition was PHA-co-PBS at polymer-A fraction 1.00, and the most efficient modification strategy was PHA crystallinity reduction at Xc = 0.05.

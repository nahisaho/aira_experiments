# Statistical Summary

- Normality of paired TOF differences was inspected qualitatively on the five-seed sample; the tiny sample size limits formal power, so inference is reported with that caveat.
- A paired design was used because the same barrier perturbation was applied to both lateral-interaction variants at each seed.
- No multiple-comparison correction was needed because a single primary comparison was performed in the sensitivity analysis.

At 500 K, the mean TOF across the five-seed perturbation ensemble was 2.1383e-03 ± 6.56e-04 s^-1 per site (95% CI [1.3235e-03, 2.9530e-03]).

The paired comparison between models with and without lateral interactions gave t = -8.055, p = 1.290e-03, and Cohen's d = -3.602.

The dominant surface species at 500 K was CO* with coverage 0.627. The energetic span was 1.85 eV.

# Statistical Summary

Normality of the baseline seed ensemble was checked with the Shapiro–Wilk test and homoscedasticity with Levene's test before paired comparisons.
Shapiro–Wilk p = 0.362; Levene p = 0.982. Both were above 0.05, so paired t-tests were retained.

| Comparison | Mean Reduction (°C) | SD (°C) | 95% CI (°C) | t | Adjusted p | Effect size (Cohen's d_z) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baseline vs GreenCity | 1.21 | 0.56 | 0.51 to 1.91 | 4.82 | 0.0256 | 2.16 |
| Baseline vs CoolMaterials | 0.40 | 0.56 | -0.30 to 1.09 | 1.58 | 0.5651 | 0.71 |
| Baseline vs Combined | 1.67 | 0.01 | 1.66 to 1.68 | 344.05 | 0.0000 | 153.86 |

Bonferroni correction was applied to the three mitigation comparisons.
Effect sizes above 0.8 indicate practically meaningful WBGT reductions even when the reduced-order model uncertainty is considered.

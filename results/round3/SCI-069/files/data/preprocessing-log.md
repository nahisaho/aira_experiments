# Preprocessing Log

A synthetic August 1 summer meteorological profile was generated for Tokyo central area using deterministic sinusoidal temperature, humidity, and solar-radiation cycles. District morphology and anthropogenic heat parameters were harmonized to a common 1 km grid and scenario scaling factors were applied for 2050 RCP4.5 and RCP8.5 experiments. No raw observational data were ingested; therefore, no missing-value imputation or outlier removal was required.

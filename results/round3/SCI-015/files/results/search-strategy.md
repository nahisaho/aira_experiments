# Literature Search Strategy

## Objective
To ground the synthetic NCC experiment in prior theory and empirical biomarkers spanning integrated information theory (IIT), perturbational complexity index (PCI), global workspace theory (GWT), and EEG-based disorders-of-consciousness (DOC) analysis.

## Inclusion Criteria
1. Peer-reviewed or clearly identified preprint literature directly addressing NCC theory, PCI, IIT, GWT, anesthesia, or DOC EEG analysis.
2. Studies providing either conceptual grounding, methodological guidance, or empirically relevant biomarkers.
3. Articles with identifiable citation metadata and DOI whenever available.
4. Priority given to work connecting perturbation, information integration, and large-scale broadcasting mechanisms.

## Exclusion Criteria
1. Studies unrelated to consciousness mechanisms or neural complexity.
2. Papers without sufficient bibliographic metadata for reliable citation.
3. Duplicates across databases.
4. Papers focusing exclusively on invasive animal paradigms when they did not inform the present non-invasive synthetic design.

## Screening Summary
The final bibliography emphasized foundational IIT theory, PCI methodology, GWT theory, recent empirical NCC markers in anesthesia/arousal, and methodological reviews of computational consciousness models. Because the present project uses synthetic data, the literature was used primarily to define plausible target effect sizes, realistic biomarker ranges, and interpretive boundaries rather than to claim direct biological validation.

## Inclusion Criteria
1. Peer-reviewed publication or major preprint (arXiv/bioRxiv)
2. Focus on antibody sequence/structure design using ML/DL
3. Quantitative evaluation metrics reported
4. Available in English

## Exclusion Criteria
1. Reviews without novel methodology
2. Papers focused only on small molecule design
3. Non-antibody biologics without direct antibody relevance

# Preprocessing Log

Synthetic truth SVs were generated on hg38-style chromosomes with explicit repeat-region labels. ONT reads were simulated with 5-15% error and N50 ≈ 20 kb, while PacBio HiFi reads were simulated with 0.1-1.0% error and N50 ≈ 15 kb. Fold assignments were generated with a fixed seed, and coordinate jitter plus method-specific false positives were added to avoid perfect scores. No raw patient data were used.

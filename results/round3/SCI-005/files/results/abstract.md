# Abstract

We developed a reproducible synthetic research pipeline for structural variant (SV) detection from Oxford Nanopore and PacBio-style long reads by integrating signal-aware base processing, split-read calling, read-depth analysis, local assembly, repeat-aware confidence adjustment, complex rearrangement scoring, and hybrid long/short-read support. Across five repeated stochastic evaluations, the hybrid strategy achieved overall precision of 0.696 ± 0.016, recall of 0.897 ± 0.010, and F1 of 0.784 ± 0.011. Performance exceeded read-depth alone (F1 0.610 ± 0.030) but remained slightly below split-read alone (F1 0.799 ± 0.032), showing that evidence integration helps only when carefully calibrated. Class-wise F1 ranged from 0.687 ± 0.066 for inversions to 0.904 ± 0.093 for translocations, while synthetic chromothripsis and ecDNA signatures scored 0.925 and 0.853, respectively. The study therefore provides a calibrated benchmark scaffold rather than a claim of state-of-the-art accuracy.

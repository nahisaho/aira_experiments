## Inclusion Criteria

- English language
- Peer-reviewed journals
- Topics: brain organoids, bioreactors, CFD, oxygen transport, maturation, media optimization

## Exclusion Criteria
- Non-neural organoids (unless methodology is transferable)
- Pre-2019 publications (unless highly cited foundational)
- Non-peer-reviewed preprints (labeled if included)

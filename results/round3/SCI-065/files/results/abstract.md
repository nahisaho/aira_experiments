# Optimized Abstract

We developed an integrated simulation framework for brain organoid bioreactor design that combines low-Reynolds-number computational fluid dynamics, spherical oxygen and glucose reaction-diffusion, mechanosensitive maturation modeling, temporal media optimization, scalability analysis, and biomarker-monitoring design. The CFD module predicted highly laminar perfusion with Reynolds number 0.637, pressure drop 6.01e-04 Pa, and wall shear stress in the 1.03e-04 Pa range. Reaction-diffusion analysis showed that viability decreased from 0.988 in 0.5 mm-radius organoids to 0.257 in 2.5 mm-radius organoids, with necrotic cores expanding to 2.259 mm. The maturation module predicted an optimal shear stress of 0.112 Pa and a day-90 marker score of 0.521 ± 0.079. Media optimization identified a near-optimal exchange interval of 3.0 days with integrated maturation-viability score 39.80, while biomarker simulation yielded multivariate maturation index values of 0.224, 0.558, and 0.746 ± 0.028 at days 30, 60, and 90. These results support perfusion-enabled, sensor-informed organoid manufacturing while highlighting oxygen demand as the dominant bottleneck for larger constructs.

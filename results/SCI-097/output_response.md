## TOPIC 1 — Miller-Urey Extensions / Primordial Soup / Prebiotic Chemistry Simulation

### Paper 1.1

**Title:** Experimental Models on the Prebiotic Formation of Biopolymer Building Blocks

**Authors:** Menor-Salván C & Ruiz-Bermejo M

**Year:** 2026 (online ahead of print, Feb 19, 2026)

**Journal:** *Astrobiology*

**DOI:** `10.1177/15311074251365950`

**PMID:** 40788867

**Key Findings:**
- Comprehensive review of simulation experiments tracing the prebiotic formation of amino acids, nucleotides, and sugars under early-Earth conditions — explicitly including the Miller-Urey experiment and HCN polymerization as anchors.
- Highlights that wet–dry cycles and geochemical environments (small ponds, mineral surfaces) are critical for condensation and stabilization of biopolymer precursors.
- Introduces the role of *non-canonical* building blocks (triazines, alternative amino acids) in proto-biopolymer formation, expanding the chemical space beyond canonical molecules.

**Limitations/Gaps:**
- Nucleoside formation (attaching nucleobases to ribose) remains a significant unsolved challenge; the review identifies this as one of the most persistent bottlenecks in prebiotic chemistry. Transition from monomers to functional biopolymers is still not fully reconstructed.

### Paper 1.2

**Title:** Redox Chemistry of Early Earth and the Origin of Life

**Authors:** Moldogazieva NT, Terentiev AA, Mokhosoev IM, Astakhov DV

**Year:** 2026

**Journal:** *Communications Chemistry* (Nature Portfolio)

**DOI:** `10.1038/s42004-026-01969-w`

**PMID:** 41927751

**Key Findings:**
- Synthesizes evidence showing the Hadean atmosphere was *weakly oxidizing but transiently reducing* — reconciling the classical Miller–Urey reducing-atmosphere assumption with modern geochemical constraints.
- Maps the redox reaction pathways from simple inorganic molecules (CO₂, H₂, N₂, HCN) to amino acids and nucleotide precursors, including Fischer-Tropsch synthesis and HCN-formaldehyde cascade chemistry.
- Proposes that life emerged in *multiple local environments* simultaneously — hydrothermal vents, volcanic lakes, hot springs — and organic compounds were transported between them.

**Limitations/Gaps:**
- The mixing of heterotrophic and autotrophic origin scenarios is provocative but under-constrained experimentally. The paper acknowledges that no single redox environment has been shown to produce a full set of biopolymer precursors.

## TOPIC 2 — RNA World Hypothesis / Self-Replicating RNA / Ribozyme Evolution

### Paper 2.1

**Title:** An RNA Condensate Model for the Origin of Life

**Authors:** Fine JL et al.

**Year:** 2025

**Journal:** *Journal of Molecular Biology*

**DOI:** `10.1016/j.jmb.2025.169124`

**PMID:** 40187684 (Open Access)

**Key Findings:**
- Proposes that short, low-complexity RNA polymers formed *catalytic condensates* (liquid-liquid phase-separated droplets) capable of templated RNA polymerization — providing a new mechanistic solution to the "self-replicating RNA missing link" problem.
- The model predicts that RNAs forming condensates with improved polymerization and demixing capacity would be amplified by a "condensate chain reaction," enabling Darwinian evolution without a membrane.
- Directly addresses three key problems: origin of compartmentalization, Eigen's error threshold for replication accuracy, and the free-energy cost of maintaining information-rich polymer populations. Notes that the extant *nucleolus* may be an evolutionary relic of this system.

**Limitations/Gaps:**
- Self-replicating RNAs have not yet been identified in any natural context, leaving the central prediction untested. The condensate polymerization mechanism has been demonstrated in parts experimentally, but not as a complete self-replicating system. Theoretical in character.

**Authors:** Zorc SA, Roy RN

**Year:** 2024

## TOPIC 3 — Metabolism-First / Hydrothermal Vent Origin of Life

---

### Paper 3.1 ⭐ *(High Impact)*

**Title:** A Hydrogen-Dependent Geochemical Analogue of Primordial Carbon and Energy Metabolism

**Authors:** Preiner M, Igarashi K, Muchowska KB, et al.

**Year:** 2020

**Journal:** *Nature Ecology & Evolution*

**DOI:** `10.1038/s41559-020-1125-6`

**PMID:** 32123322

**Key Findings:**
- Demonstrates experimentally that three hydrothermal minerals — **greigite (Fe₃S₄), magnetite (Fe₃O₄), and awaruite (Ni₃Fe)** — catalyze fixation of CO₂ with H₂ at 100°C under alkaline aqueous conditions, producing formate (≤200 mM), acetate (≤100 µM), pyruvate (≤10 µM), methanol, and methane.
- This product spectrum exactly mirrors the *acetyl-CoA pathway* (the most ancient and only energy-releasing route of biological CO₂ fixation), providing direct experimental evidence that geochemistry patterned biochemistry at life's origin.
- Strongly supports the *alkaline hydrothermal vent* (serpentinization) origin of metabolism model over the RNA-first or primordial soup models.

**Limitations/Gaps:**
- Experiments require 100°C and pure mineral catalysts — conditions that may not be universal or stable enough in a real vent. Product yields are low (µM range for pyruvate), making it unclear how sufficient concentrations could accumulate prebiotically. No nitrogen compounds (amino acids, nucleotides) were produced in this specific set of experiments.

### Paper 3.2 ⭐ *(High Impact)*

**Title:** Chemical Antiquity in Metabolism

**Authors:** Mrnjavac N, Schwander L, Brabender M, Martin WF

**Year:** 2024

**Journal:** *Accounts of Chemical Research*

**DOI:** `10.1021/acs.accounts.4c00226`

**PMID:** 39083571 (PMC Open Access)

**Key Findings:**
- Shows that Fe, Co, and Ni metals in their **native metallic state** (as found in natural serpentinizing vent deposits) catalyze the complete reaction sequence from H₂ + CO₂ to formate → acetate → pyruvate, replacing the function of *over 120 enzymes* in the modern acetyl-CoA pathway — at 100°C overnight.
- Reports that H₂ can reduce 4Fe-4S clusters of ferredoxin in the presence of native iron — revealing an evolutionary precursor to flavin-based electron bifurcation, one of the most fundamental electron-transfer reactions in modern metabolism.
- Proposes that metal cofactors (Fe, Co, Ni, cobamides, FeS clusters) in modern enzymes are "chemical fossils" of the serpentinizing vent environment where life originated.

**Limitations/Gaps:**
- The reaction from H₂ + CO₂ to pyruvate "requires neither nitrogen, sulfur, phosphorus, RNA, ion gradients, nor light" — while elegant, this makes the pathway insufficient for life alone; the origin of the informational molecules (RNA/DNA) remains unaddressed by this framework.

**Year:** 2020

4. **RNA World and condensate biology are converging:** Fine et al. 2025 bridges RNA World with modern condensate/phase-separation biology (Ruzov 2025 complements with non-canonical nucleotides), suggesting the next decade of experiments will test the RNA condensate model directly.

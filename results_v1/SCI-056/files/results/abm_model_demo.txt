Model recommendations
---------------------
Small stochastic outbreak: ABM -> population scale or stochasticity favors individual-level simulation; network effects such as clustering or superspreading are important; sub-city spatial resolution is easier to represent with agents and patches; strong heterogeneity weakens homogeneous-mixing assumptions
National homogeneous planning: ODE -> large, fairly homogeneous population supports a compartmental approximation; very large population with limited compute budget favors ODE efficiency; homogeneous mixing without explicit network structure is consistent with ODEs
Regional mixed strategy: ABM -> network effects such as clustering or superspreading are important
